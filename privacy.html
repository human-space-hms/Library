隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日　最後更新：2026 年 11 月 18 日

這份政策說明 Human Space（以下稱「本站」）如何蒐集、使用與保護你的個人資料。內容依臺灣《個人資料保護法》第 8 條的告知事項撰寫，並納入歐盟 GDPR 要求告知的項目。

1. 誰負責處理你的資料

蒐集與處理者：張德安，以 Human Space 名義經營。聯絡信箱：itshumanspace@gmail.com。目前未指定資料保護長。

2. 我們蒐集什麼、為什麼、保存多久

情境蒐集的資料目的與法律依據保存期間接收方

贊助與購買 你在 Portaly 提供的姓名或暱稱、Email、金額與留言。付款資料由 Portaly 與其金流商處理，我們看不到完整卡號 處理贊助與購買、致謝與帳務；履行交易及法令要求 依法令規定的帳務保存期限 Portaly

測驗的作答與結果在你的瀏覽器中計算，我們不會收到。

3. Cookie 與類似技術

本站不使用 cookie 追蹤你。本站使用瀏覽器的儲存空間（localStorage）記住你選擇的語言，這是為了提供你要求的功能，不用於追蹤。

本站使用 Cloudflare Web Analytics 做匿名流量統計。依 Cloudflare 的說明，它不使用 cookie 或 localStorage，也不透過 IP 位址或瀏覽器資訊辨識個別使用者。

Portaly 頁面由 Portaly 提供，其中的 cookie 與追蹤工具適用 Portaly 的政策。

4. 資料會分享給誰，是否跨境傳輸

我們只在上表所列的目的下，與提供服務的業者分享資料：Kit、Google、Portaly、GitHub、Cloudflare。這些業者可能在臺灣以外的國家處理資料，包括美國。跨境傳輸時，依各業者公開的隱私與資料保護機制提供保護。除法律要求外，我們不會把你的資料提供給其他第三方。

5. 你的權利

依臺灣個資法第 3 條，你可以向我們：

  • 查詢或請求閱覽你的個人資料
  • 請求製給複製本
  • 請求補充或更正
  • 請求停止蒐集、處理或利用
  • 請求刪除

如果歐盟 GDPR 或其他當地法律適用於你，你還可以請求限制處理、反對處理、請求資料可攜，並隨時撤回同意。撤回不影響撤回前已進行的處理。你也可以向所在地的資料保護主管機關提出申訴。

行使權利請寄信到 itshumanspace@gmail.com。我們可能需要確認你的身分，並會在 30 日內回覆。

6. 不提供資料的影響

是否提供個人資料由你自由選擇。不提供 Email，就無法收到電子報；不提供洽詢資料，我們就無法回覆合作詢問。

7. 兒童與青少年

本站並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，我們不會刻意蒐集他們的個人資料。如果你發現孩子提供了資料，請來信，我們會刪除。未滿 18 歲的使用者，請在家長或法定代理人同意下訂閱。

8. 資訊安全

我們採取合理的措施保護資料，例如限制可存取資料的人員，並使用提供加密連線的服務。沒有任何網路服務能保證百分之百安全。如果發生可能影響你的資料外洩，我們會依適用法律通知你與主管機關。

9. 行銷信件、出售資料與自動化決策

每封電子報都有退訂連結。我們不出售你的個人資料，也不對你做具有法律或類似重大影響的自動化決策。

10. 營運者變更

若未來由公司承接 Human Space 的營運，我們可能將本站與相關資料移轉給承接者，並事先在本站公告；資料的使用目的不變。

11. 政策變更

我們可能更新這份政策，更新後的版本會在最上方標示新的日期。重大變更會另外公告，需要你同意的變更會先取得同意。

12. 聯絡我們

任何隱私相關問題或申訴，請寄信到 itshumanspace@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Controller: 張德安, operating as Human Space. Contact email: itshumanspace@gmail.com. We have not appointed a data protection officer.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

The Site does not use cookies to track you. It uses your browser’s local storage to remember the language you choose. This supports a feature you asked for and is not used for tracking.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics for anonymous traffic statistics. According to Cloudflare, it does not use cookies or local storage, and it does not identify individual visitors by IP address or browser details.

The Portaly page is provided by Portaly, and its cookies and tracking tools are covered by Portaly’s policies.

4. Who we share data with, and transfers abroad

We share data only for the purposes in the table above, with the providers that deliver those services: Kit, Google, Portaly and GitHub, and Cloudflare. These providers may process data outside Taiwan, including in the United States. When data is transferred abroad, protection depends on the privacy and data protection measures each provider publishes. We do not give your data to other third parties except where the law requires it.

5. Your rights

Under Article 3 of Taiwan’s Personal Data Protection Act, you may ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold, or let you review it
  • give you a copy
  • add to or correct it
  • stop collecting, processing or using it
  • delete it

If the EU GDPR or another local law applies to you, you may also ask us to restrict processing, object to processing, move your data to another service, and withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

To use these rights, email itshumanspace@gmail.com. We may need to confirm who you are, and we will reply within 30 days.

6. If you choose not to give data

Giving us personal data is your choice. Without an email address, we cannot send you the newsletter. Without your enquiry details, we cannot reply to a collaboration request.

7. Children and teenagers

The Site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you find that a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it. If you are under 18, please subscribe only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, such as limiting who can access it and using services that offer encrypted connections. No online service can promise complete security. If a data breach is likely to affect you, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Marketing email, sale of data and automated decisions

Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

10. Change of operator

If a company takes over the running of Human Space in future, we may transfer the Site and related data to that company. We will announce this on the Site beforehand, and the purposes for which data is used will not change.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The updated version will show a new date at the top. We will announce significant changes, and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

12. Contact us

For any privacy question or complaint, email itshumanspace@gmail.com.
