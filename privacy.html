生效日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日　最後更新：2026 年 11 月 18 日

本政策說明 Human Space（以下稱「本站」）如何蒐集、使用及保護你的個人資料。內容依臺灣《個人資料保護法》第 8 條規定的告知事項撰寫，並納入歐盟 GDPR 要求告知的項目。

蒐集與處理者：張德安，以 Human Space 名義經營。

聯絡信箱：itshumanspace@gmail.com。

3. Cookie 與類似技術

本站不使用 Cookie 追蹤你的瀏覽行為。本站會使用瀏覽器的儲存空間（localStorage）記住你選擇的語言。這項功能是為了提供你所需的使用體驗，不會用於追蹤。

本站使用 Cloudflare Web Analytics 進行匿名流量統計。根據 Cloudflare 的說明，這項服務不使用 Cookie 或 localStorage，也不會透過 IP 位址或瀏覽器資訊辨識個別使用者。

Portaly 頁面由 Portaly 提供，其 Cookie 與追蹤工具適用 Portaly 自身的隱私權政策。

4. 資料會分享給誰，以及是否會跨境傳輸

我們只會基於上表所列的目的，與提供相關服務的業者分享資料，包括 Kit、Google、Portaly、GitHub、Cloudflare。

這些業者可能會在臺灣以外的國家或地區處理資料，包括美國。資料跨境傳輸時，會依各業者公開的隱私權與資料保護機制提供保護。除非法律另有要求，否則我們不會將你的資料提供給其他第三方。

5. 你的權利

依臺灣個資法第 3 條，你可以向我們提出以下請求：

  • 查詢或請求閱覽你的個人資料
  • 請求提供個人資料的複製本
  • 請求補充或更正個人資料
  • 請求停止蒐集、處理或利用個人資料
  • 請求刪除個人資料

如果歐盟 GDPR 或其他當地法律適用於你，你還可以請求限制資料處理、反對資料處理、請求資料可攜，並隨時撤回同意。撤回同意不會影響撤回前已進行的資料處理。你也可以向所在地的資料保護主管機關提出申訴。

如需行使上述權利，請寄信至 itshumanspace@gmail.com。我們可能需要先確認你的身分，並會在 30 日內回覆。

6. 不提供資料的影響

你可以自行決定是否提供個人資料。不提供 Email，我們就無法寄送電子報給你；不提供洽詢資料，我們就無法回覆你的合作詢問。

7. 兒童與青少年

本站並非以 13 歲以下兒童為對象設計，我們也不會刻意蒐集他們的個人資料。如果你發現孩子曾向本站提供個人資料，請來信告知，我們會將資料刪除。

未滿 18 歲的使用者，請先取得家長或法定代理人的同意，再訂閱電子報。

8. 資訊安全

我們會採取合理措施保護你的資料，例如限制資料存取權限，並使用提供加密連線的服務。

不過，沒有任何網路服務能保證百分之百的安全。如果發生可能影響你個人資料的外洩事件，我們會依適用法律規定通知你及相關主管機關。

9. 行銷信件、資料出售與自動化決策

每封電子報都會附上退訂連結。我們不會出售你的個人資料，也不會透過自動化決策對你做出具有法律效力或類似重大影響的決定。

10. 營運者變更

如果未來由公司承接 Human Space 的營運，我們可能會將本站及相關資料移轉給承接公司，並事先在本站公告。移轉後，資料的使用目的不會改變。

11. 政策變更

我們可能會不定期更新本政策。更新後的版本會在頁面最上方標示新的日期。若有重大變更，我們會另外公告；如變更內容需要取得你的同意，我們會事先徵求你的同意。

12. 聯絡我們

如果你對隱私權有任何疑問或申訴，請寄信至 itshumanspace@gmail.com。

3. Cookies and similar technologies

The Site does not use cookies to track your browsing activity. It uses your browser’s local storage to remember the language you select. This feature supports the experience you requested and is not used for tracking.

The Site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for anonymous traffic statistics. According to Cloudflare, this service does not use cookies or local storage, and does not identify individual visitors through their IP addresses or browser information.

The Portaly page is provided by Portaly. Its cookies and tracking tools are covered by Portaly’s own privacy policy.

4. Who we share your data with and international transfers

We share data only for the purposes in the table above, and only with the providers that deliver those services: Kit, Google, Portaly and GitHub (plus Cloudflare for site analytics).

These providers may process data outside Taiwan, including in the United States. When data is transferred internationally, it is protected under the privacy and data protection measures published by each provider. We do not share your data with other third parties unless required by law.

5. Your rights

Under Article 3 of Taiwan’s Personal Data Protection Act, you may ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal data we hold or let you review it
  • Provide you with a copy of your personal data
  • Add to or correct your personal data
  • Stop collecting, processing or using your personal data
  • Delete your personal data

If the EU GDPR or another local law applies to you, you may also ask us to restrict processing, object to processing, request data portability, and withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect any processing carried out before the withdrawal. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority where you live.

To exercise these rights, email us at itshumanspace@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity and will respond within 30 days.

6. If you choose not to provide data

Providing personal data is your choice. Without an email address, we cannot send you our newsletter. Without your enquiry details, we cannot respond to your collaboration request.

7. Children and teenagers

The Site is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you discover that a child has provided personal data to us, please email us and we will delete it.

If you are under 18, please obtain consent from a parent or legal guardian before subscribing to the newsletter.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data. These include limiting access to personal data and using services that provide encrypted connections.

However, no online service can guarantee complete security. If a data breach is likely to affect your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

9. Marketing emails, sale of data and automated decisions

Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. We do not sell your personal data or make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

10. Change of operator

If a company takes over the operation of Human Space in future, we may transfer the Site and related data to that company. We will announce the change on the Site beforehand, and the purposes for which your data is used will remain unchanged.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will show a new date at the top of the page. We will announce significant changes separately. If a change requires your consent, we will ask for it beforehand.

12. Contact us

If you have any questions or complaints about privacy, please email us at itshumanspace@gmail.com.
